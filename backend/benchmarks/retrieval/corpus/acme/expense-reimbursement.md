# Expense Reimbursement

## Receipts

Itemised receipts are required for any claim above 25 dollars. Card statements are not accepted in place of receipts.

## Approval

Claims above 500 dollars need approval from a budget holder before reimbursement is processed. Claims are submitted in Expensify within sixty days.

## Meals

When travelling, meals are reimbursed up to 75 dollars per day including tips. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Client entertainment is pre-approved by the account director.
