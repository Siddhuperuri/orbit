# Price List

## Plans

ORB-STARTER is free for up to three seats.

ORB-PRO-12 is the Professional plan on a twelve-month term at 49 dollars per seat per month.

ORB-ENT-36 is the Enterprise plan on a thirty-six-month term at 39 dollars per seat per month, including SSO and audit export.

## Discounts

Orders above 200 seats receive a 10 percent volume discount. Non-profit organisations receive 30 percent off ORB-PRO-12.
