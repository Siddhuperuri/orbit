# Data Retention Schedule

## Operational data

Application logs are retained for 90 days. Database backups are retained for 35 days and are encrypted at rest.

## Customer data

Customer data is deleted within 30 days of contract termination. Right-to-erasure requests under GDPR Article 17 are completed within 30 days of verification.

## Legal hold

A legal hold issued by counsel suspends deletion for the records it names until counsel releases it.
