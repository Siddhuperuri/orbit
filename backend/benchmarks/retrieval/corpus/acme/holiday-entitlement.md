# Holiday Entitlement

## Allowance

Everyone receives twenty-eight vacation days per calendar year plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata allowance.

## Carrying over

Up to five unused vacation days may be carried over, and must be taken before 31 March. Anything beyond five is forfeited unless your manager approved an exception in writing.

## Booking

Book vacation in Workday at least two weeks ahead. Vacation cannot be booked during the year-end change freeze from 15 December to 5 January without director approval.
