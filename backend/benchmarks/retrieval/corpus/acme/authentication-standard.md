# Authentication Standard

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Long passphrases are encouraged over complex short passwords. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; they are changed only when compromise is suspected.

## Multi-factor authentication

MFA is mandatory for every account. A YubiKey 5C is issued on the first day; a TOTP authenticator app is the fallback if the key is lost. SMS codes are not permitted as a second factor.
