# Remote Work Policy

## Equipment

A one-off home office stipend of 600 euros covers a desk, chair, and monitor. Internet is reimbursed at 30 euros per month.

## Working abroad

Working from another country is allowed for up to 30 days per calendar year with approval from your manager and the people team, for tax and immigration reasons.

## Core hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 CET, when meetings may be scheduled.
