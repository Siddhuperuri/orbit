# Business Travel

## Booking

All flights, trains, and hotels are booked through Navan so that travellers can be located in an emergency.

## Flights

Economy class for flights under six hours; premium economy for longer flights. Rail is preferred for journeys under four hours.

## Hotels

Hotels are capped at 250 dollars per night, or 350 dollars per night in London, New York, and San Francisco. Conference hotels booked by the organiser are exempt from the cap.
