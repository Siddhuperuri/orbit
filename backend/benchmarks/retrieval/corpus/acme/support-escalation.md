# Customer Support Escalation

## Priorities

P1 means production is down for the customer: first response within one hour, around the clock. P2 is a major feature impaired: response within four hours. P3 is a question or minor issue: next business day.

## Escalation

Tickets unresolved after two hours at tier one move to tier two. Enterprise accounts are escalated to their account manager; for the EMEA region that is Priya Okonkwo.
