# Parental Leave Policy

## Entitlement

Employees who become parents through birth, adoption, or surrogacy receive sixteen weeks of parental leave at full salary. The entitlement is the same for every parent regardless of gender, and it applies from the first day of employment.

Leave may begin up to four weeks before the expected due date. For adoption, it begins on the date of placement.

## Requesting leave

Submit the request in Workday at least eight weeks in advance, attaching the expected due date or the placement letter. Your people partner confirms dates within five working days.

## Returning

Parents may take a phased return: four days per week at full salary for the first month back. Up to ten keeping-in-touch days may be taken during leave without ending it.
