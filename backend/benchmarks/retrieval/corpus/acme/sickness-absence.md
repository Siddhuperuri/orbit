# Sickness Absence

## Reporting an absence

If you are unwell, notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. Record the absence in Workday on your return.

## Evidence

You may self-certify for up to five consecutive calendar days. From the sixth day a fit note from a doctor is required and must be uploaded to Workday.

## Sick pay

Full salary is paid for twenty working days of sickness in any rolling twelve-month period. After that, statutory sick pay applies. Long-term absence is supported by an occupational health referral.
